## Three-or-more realistic providers per island

“Three options” below means independent, genuinely operating providers or sites with a plausible service; it does **not** mean three confirmed cars for the date.

- **Fuerteventura:** PlusCar (port delivery claim + FUE airport inventory seen), CICAR (port desk), TopCar (port desk), AutoReisen (FUE airport), Enterprise (FUE airport).
- **Lanzarote:** CICAR and TopCar (cruise-port offices), PlusCar (port delivery claim), AutoReisen and Enterprise (ACE airport).
- **Gran Canaria:** CICAR (Santa Catalina port), PlusCar (port-delivery claim), TopCar (LPA), Avis (airport and Dársena Africa listing), plus other airport firms (Enterprise/Europcar/Avis etc.).
- **Madeira:** RentADrive (port pickup), Madeira Hot Wheels (harbour delivery claim), Madeira Rent (Funchal/Airport), Madeira Car Hire (Funchal address delivery), GAR105/Funchal Drive (additional local companies).
- **Tenerife:** current TopCar booking, CICAR (port/TFN/TFS), AutoReisen (airport), PlusCar (airport service), and international airport providers. No additional operator is needed to satisfy a search count when there is already a valid booking; alternatives remain comparison/contingency only.

## Operator / fleet independence

CICAR joined the Cabrera Medina Group; Autos Cabrera Medina and CICAR must be counted as one group for fleet redundancy. Do not list both as two independent backups just because separate web pages exist. Other brands require individual legal-entity/group checks if evaluating fleet capacity. PlusCar Canarias site/legal entity differs from airport desk concession labels in some comparisons; confirm the voucher company name.

## What to reserve after user decision

1. **Fuerteventura:** recheck PlusCar age-correct price, port-specific pickup/return, class, and written key-return; compare with CICAR/TopCar port calendar once Jan opens.
2. **Lanzarote:** verify that selected Arrecife port operator can hand over after actual disembarkation and return before planned port buffer; no need to transfer to ACE unless a date-specific quote motivates it.
3. **Gran Canaria:** prioritize port service; if LPA, budget both taxi legs or bus and retain an earlier final-return control time.
4. **Madeira:** reserve only after confirming exact cruise-port meeting point, full price including €30 after-hours component, fuel, deposits, group/insurance and the 21:30 location handback.
5. **Tenerife:** no new booking action proposed; compare existing voucher vehicle group against luggage, retain TopCar unless user chooses a demonstrably better alternative.

## Reviews and caveats

- Which? survey/rating is an independent consumer comparison for CICAR, but does not guarantee an individual cruise-port shift.
- Trustpilot and traveler-forum reviews show anecdotal experience for CICAR, TopCar and RentADrive; both good and bad reports can coexist. They are signals to inspect fuel, dents, queue, deposit and handback, not substitute for the signed terms.
- A date-specific price on a form is not the same as an issued booking voucher; a generic “airport/port delivery” statement is not proof of staffed cruise-port handover on a certain hour.
